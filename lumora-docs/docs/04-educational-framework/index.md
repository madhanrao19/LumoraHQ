# Educational Framework

## Purpose

This book defines the pedagogical principles behind both human-authored and AI-assisted learning experiences. [AI Agents Handbook](../16-ai-agents-handbook/index.md#not-yet-decided) deferred the AI Tutor's teaching behavior here — this is where that gets resolved.

## Status

Version: 1.1 foundation draft. Grounds pedagogy in existing Constitution principles; teaching-methodology specifics are flagged open below rather than invented.

## Pedagogical principles

Directly from the [Constitution](../00-constitution/index.md#non-negotiable-principles):

- **Student-first** (principle 1) — every learning-experience decision is judged by whether it improves understanding, confidence, retention, or access for the student, not by what's technically impressive.
- **Learning before technology** (principle 2) — a feature only earns its place if it improves understanding, confidence, retention, or access.
- **Accuracy** (principle 6) — educational content and AI responses must be grounded, reviewed, and traceable; a confident wrong answer is worse than an honest "I don't know."
- **Accessibility by default** (principle 7) — usable by learners with different abilities, languages, devices, and internet conditions.

## AI Tutor's pedagogical role

[AI Safety Principles](../06-ai-development-bible/ai-safety-principles.md) requirement 5 ("AI must clearly say when it does not know") is already binding and is itself a pedagogical stance, not just a safety one — intellectual honesty models good learning behavior.

!!! note "Not yet decided"
    The Tutor's teaching methodology (Socratic questioning vs. direct instruction, how it scaffolds a student who's stuck, when it prompts vs. tells) is not decided. [AI Agents Handbook](../16-ai-agents-handbook/index.md) flagged this as belonging here; it remains open pending an ADR.

## Assessment philosophy

The [Roadmap](../25-roadmap/index.md) Phase 1 includes an Assessment Engine. This book's principle: assessment should measure understanding, not just recall — consistent with "learning before technology" above. Structural detail (question types, scoring mechanics, question bank organization) belongs to [Curriculum Framework](../05-curriculum-framework/index.md), not this book.

## Curriculum alignment stance

Same stance as [Vision & Philosophy](../01-vision-philosophy/index.md#curriculum-aligned-not-curriculum-bound): curriculum references (e.g. Malaysian curricula) are used for alignment, not as an exclusive basis. Structural detail belongs to [Curriculum Framework](../05-curriculum-framework/index.md).
