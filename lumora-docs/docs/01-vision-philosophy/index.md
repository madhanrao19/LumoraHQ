# Vision & Philosophy

## Purpose

This book states the "why" behind Lumora Academy — the mission and the reasoning behind the principles the [Constitution](../00-constitution/index.md) makes binding. The Constitution is the decision rulebook; this book is the reasoning that produced it.

## Status

Version: 1.1 foundation draft.

## Mission

Lumora Academy is an AI-native learning platform designed to provide curriculum-aligned, personalized, safe, and future-ready education experiences for students, parents, teachers, and content teams.

## Why AI-native, not AI-bolted-on

One-size-fits-all instruction leaves gaps for learners who need more time, a different explanation, or a different pace. AI can close those gaps by personalizing the experience — but only if it stays bounded and human-accountable, which is exactly what the Constitution's principles 2 and 3 (learning before technology; AI assists, humans remain accountable) require. Lumora treats AI as core infrastructure for personalization, not a bolt-on feature, while keeping humans responsible for what gets published and taught.

## Curriculum-aligned, not curriculum-bound

Per repository convention (`lumora-docs/CLAUDE.md`), Malaysian curriculum references are used only as an alignment reference where legally and contextually appropriate — Lumora Academy is not built exclusively around one national curriculum. The philosophy is alignment for relevance, not lock-in to a single syllabus.
