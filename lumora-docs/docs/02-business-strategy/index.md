# Business Strategy

## Purpose

This book will define Lumora Academy's revenue model, target customer segments, competitive positioning, and go-to-market plan. None of that exists yet — this page states the one constraint that's already binding on whatever gets decided here, rather than inventing a strategy that hasn't been made.

## Status

Version: 0.1 — placeholder. No business strategy has been written. Do not treat the absence of content here as "no strategy needed" — it means this is genuinely undecided.

## The one constraint already established

Per the [Constitution](../00-constitution/index.md#decision-rule):

> When there is conflict between speed, profit, convenience, and student safety, student safety wins.

Whatever revenue model or go-to-market plan eventually lands here is bound by that rule — it isn't optional once written.

## Not yet decided

- Revenue model (subscription, institutional licensing, freemium, or a mix).
- Target customer segments — the [Roadmap](../25-roadmap/index.md) Phase 3 items ("School edition," "Marketplace") hint at an eventual B2B/institutional angle alongside individual families, but nothing is decided.
- Pricing.
- Funding and runway.
- Go-to-market sequencing and competitive positioning.

Each of these should be resolved and recorded here — as prose backed by an ADR for anything binding, per [Development Standards](../08-development-standards/index.md#feature-workflow) — not assumed from the roadmap's feature list.
