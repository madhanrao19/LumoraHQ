# Lumora Constitution

## Purpose

The Lumora Constitution is the highest-level decision document for Lumora Academy. It defines the principles that guide product, education, engineering, AI, security, privacy, operations, and business decisions.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Students come first.** Every major decision must improve or protect the student learning experience.
2. **Learning comes before technology.** Technology is only valuable when it improves understanding, confidence, retention, or access.
3. **AI assists; humans remain accountable.** Published educational content must remain under human responsibility.
4. **Child safety is mandatory.** The platform must be designed to protect children from harmful content, unsafe interaction, manipulation, privacy abuse, and inappropriate AI behavior.
5. **Privacy is a product feature.** Data collection must be minimal, purposeful, protected, and explainable.
6. **Accuracy matters.** Educational content and AI responses must be grounded, reviewed, and traceable.
7. **Accessibility is default.** The product must be usable by learners with different abilities, languages, devices, and internet conditions.
8. **Documentation is part of the product.** Undocumented features are incomplete.
9. **Architecture must remain modular.** The platform should evolve without major rewrites.
10. **Vendor lock-in must be minimized.** AI providers, cloud providers, and infrastructure choices must remain replaceable where practical.

## Decision rule

When there is conflict between speed, profit, convenience, and student safety, student safety wins.

When there is conflict between impressive technology and learning value, learning value wins.

When there is conflict between short-term delivery and long-term maintainability, document the trade-off before proceeding.

## Naming rule

The system name is **Lumora Academy**. Do not include external curriculum authority names in the product name. Curriculum references may be mentioned only as alignment references where legally and contextually appropriate.
