# AI Safety Principles

## Core rule

AI must be useful, bounded, reviewable, and safe for children.

## Requirements

1. AI should answer from approved Lumora content by default.
2. AI-generated educational content requires human review before publication.
3. AI responses must be logged for audit and improvement.
4. AI must not fabricate curriculum facts.
5. AI must clearly say when it does not know.
6. Parents and authorized staff should have appropriate visibility into child AI interactions.
7. Unsafe content must be blocked, escalated, or redirected.
8. Provider abstraction is mandatory so models can be changed later.
